\## 🔍 Detailed Analysis



\### 1. Vercel — vercel.com

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Dark, high-contrast, technical precision

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Full-width dark background with subtle grid texture

\- Bold white headline with gradient accent on key word

\- Animated globe/network visualization as hero visual

\- CTA: "Start Deploying" — action-oriented, no friction



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Extreme performance — page feels instant

\- Motion is purposeful: elements reveal on scroll, never distracting

\- Social proof placed immediately below hero (logos of major companies)

\- Pricing is transparent and accessible from nav



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Use dark backgrounds with subtle grid/dot textures. Animate the hero visual subtly. Keep CTAs short and action-driven.



\---



\### 2. Linear — linear.app

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Dark minimal, editorial, refined typography

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Cinematic product screenshot as hero (not illustration)

\- Extremely tight, confident typography — large, bold, no fluff

\- Subtle gradient orbs in background for depth

\- Two CTAs: "Get started" (primary) + "Watch video" (secondary)



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Typography does the heavy lifting — no need for excessive visuals

\- Video-to-frames scroll concept: product UI is revealed section by section as user scrolls

\- Features shown in context (not just icons + text)

\- Micro-interactions on hover are smooth and feel premium



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* The \*\*video-to-frames scroll\*\* pattern is inspired by Linear. Show the resume builder UI being used in real-time as user scrolls down.



\---



\### 3. Notion AI — notion.so

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Clean, light, approachable, editorial

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Light background with colorful, playful illustrations

\- Conversational headline: "Write. Plan. Organize. With AI."

\- Animated text cycling through use cases

\- CTA: "Get Notion free" — zero friction, clear value



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Extremely accessible — not intimidating for non-tech users

\- Sections tell a story: problem → solution → social proof → pricing

\- Feature sections alternate layout (text left/right) to maintain visual interest

\- Trust signals: "Used by 30M+ users"



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Alternate section layouts. Use cycling/animated text in hero to show multiple resume use cases.



\---



\### 4. Midjourney — midjourney.com

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Dark, artistic, immersive, community-first

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Full-bleed gallery of AI-generated images as hero background

\- Minimal text overlay — lets the product speak

\- Single CTA: "Get Started"



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- The product IS the hero visual — no mockups needed

\- Gallery grid creates immediate "wow" factor

\- Dark overlay with blur creates depth and legibility

\- Community angle is front and center (Discord integration)



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Use actual resume outputs as hero visuals. Show beautiful, real resumes generated by the tool.



\---



\### 5. Runway ML — runwayml.com

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Dark, cinematic, video-first

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Auto-playing video reel in the background (muted, looped)

\- Bold white text overlay

\- "Try for free" CTA with high contrast



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Video background immediately communicates what the product does

\- Transitions between feature sections use full-screen video clips

\- Color palette: deep black + electric green accent

\- Motion is core to the brand — not just decoration



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Use video-to-frames scroll to show resume creation process frame by frame — similar to how Runway shows video generation.



\---



\### 6. Framer — framer.com

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Bold, expressive, design-forward

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Animated site examples displayed in a floating browser mockup

\- Gradient mesh background (purple/blue)

\- "Build your site in minutes" — clear value prop

\- CTA: "Start for free"



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Interactive demo available immediately on landing

\- Template gallery shown before any feature explanation

\- Pricing visible in navbar — builds trust

\- "Made with Framer" social proof is creative and on-brand



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Show resume templates in a floating browser/card mockup. Let users preview before signing up.



\---



\### 7. Pitch — pitch.com

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Warm, collaborative, modern

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Light background with colorful product screenshots

\- Headline: "Collaborate on beautiful presentations"

\- Shows team collaboration UI prominently



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Collaboration angle differentiates from solo tools

\- Feature highlights use short, punchy sentences (not paragraphs)

\- Testimonial section uses real photos and company logos

\- Free tier is prominently mentioned — reduces signup friction



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* If Draftdeckai has collaboration features, lead with them. Short feature descriptions > long paragraphs.



\---



\### 8. Tome — tome.app

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Dark, futuristic, AI-native

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Full-screen dark background with glowing AI visual

\- "The AI-powered format for work" — broad but intriguing

\- Animated demo of AI generating a presentation

\- CTA: "Try Tome for free"



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- AI generation is shown live — not just described

\- Smooth section transitions using scroll-triggered animations

\- Features are shown as short videos, not static screenshots

\- Minimal nav — keeps focus on conversion



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Show the AI generating a resume live in the hero. Animate the text appearing in resume fields.



\---



\### 9. Gamma — gamma.app

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Clean, bold, conversion-focused

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- "Make a presentation, document, or webpage — instantly"

\- Input box right in the hero: "Describe what you want to make"

\- Immediate product interaction — no sign-up required to try



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Lowest friction of all audited sites — try before signup

\- Output examples shown as cards below the hero

\- Feature sections are benefit-focused, not feature-focused

\- "No design skills needed" messaging — removes fear



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Add a "Try it now" input in the hero section. Let users enter their job title and see a sample resume generate instantly.



\---



\### 10. Loom — loom.com

\*\*Aesthetic:\*\* Warm, human, approachable

\*\*Hero Section:\*\*

\- Video playing in hero showing real use case

\- "Record and share video messages"

\- Two CTAs: "Get Loom Free" + "Watch how it works"



\*\*What Works Well:\*\*

\- Human faces in product screenshots build trust

\- Use case sections are job-role specific (for sales, engineering, etc.)

\- "How it works" is 3 simple steps — not overwhelming

\- Free tier CTA is repeated multiple times on the page



\*\*Key Takeaway for Draftdeckai:\*\* Add job-role specific sections ("For freshers", "For senior engineers", "For designers"). Show real user success stories.

\## 🚀 Recommended Direction for Draftdeckai



Based on this audit, the premium landing page redesign should:



1\. \*\*Hero:\*\* Dark background + animated resume being generated by AI in real time

2\. \*\*Scroll Experience:\*\* Video-to-frames reveal of resume builder UI (inspired by Linear/Runway)

3\. \*\*Typography:\*\* Large, confident headlines — short and punchy

4\. \*\*CTA Strategy:\*\* "Build your resume free" — action-oriented, zero friction

5\. \*\*Social Proof:\*\* Company logos + user count immediately below hero

6\. \*\*Feature Sections:\*\* Short videos showing each feature, not static screenshots

7\. \*\*Template Gallery:\*\* Show beautiful resume outputs early on the page

8\. \*\*Try Before Signup:\*\* Input box where users can enter job title and preview a resume
